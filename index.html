Projects

Charlotte Rain-Skip Check (Python, Data)

Status: v0.2 complete

Uses 10 years of NOAA weather data to check how often a sprinkler timer in Charlotte waters right after real rain (15.4% of waterings). A rain-aware schedule saves 9.9% of waterings, or 16.9% if it also knows that day's rain. A machine learning rain model barely beat chance (AUC 0.61) and was no more accurate than always guessing no rain.
