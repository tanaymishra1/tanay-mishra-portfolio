Engineering Trade-Off Simulator

Python • v2.0

What It Is

A Python program I wrote that compares design options. You rate each design's speed, torque and cost from 1 to 10, decide how much each one matters, and it ranks them with a score out of 100.

Why I Made It

I got the idea from FRC robotics, where making one thing better usually makes something else worse. A faster drivetrain has less torque, and better parts cost more. I wanted a simple way to compare options like that, and I also wanted practice writing classes in Python.

How It Works

  • You give speed, torque and cost a weight, and they get scaled to add up to 1
  • Each design is a Design object that works out its own score
  • Speed and torque add to the score and cost takes away from it
  • Torque under 5 costs a point and fails the torque check
  • The score goes from 0, the worst design possible with your weights, to 100, the best
  • Designs are ranked, and each one gets an evaluation and a design focus

What Changed in v2.0

The first version used a cutoff of 6 for "well-balanced", but with equal weights the best possible score was about 6.3, so almost nothing reached it. Version 2 scores out of 100 instead. I also moved the weights out of global variables, fixed the place names (it used to print "21th"), and added tests that GitHub runs every time I push.

What's Next

I'd like to add saved weight presets for different goals. The Trade-Off Visualizer is the web version, with sliders and a chart.
